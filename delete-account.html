Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Play Indoor (app.play.indoor) Developer: Play Indoor Last Updated: September 23, 2026

At Play Indoor, we believe in giving you complete ownership and control over your personal data. In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data and Account Deletion policies, this page provides instructions on how to request the deletion of your Play Indoor account and associated data.

Important Notice Before Deletion

Deleting your account is permanent. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. Any active session passes, saved venue favorites, reward points, and profile history will be permanently erased.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion using either of the two methods below:

1
In-App Account Deletion (Instant & Recommended)

If you have the Play Indoor application installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the Play Indoor app on your device.
  2. Log in to the account you want to delete (if not already logged in).
  3. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Tap on Settings (or the gear icon).
  5. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  6. Read the confirmation warning and tap Confirm Delete.

Your account, authentication record, and profile data will be deleted immediately from our active system.

2
Web / Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot log in to your account, you can submit a manual deletion request by email:

  • Recipient: wajahtali55@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Play Indoor
  • Required Details in Body:
    • The email address or phone number registered with your Play Indoor account
    • Your full registered name

For your security, we may send a verification email or SMS to verify account ownership before processing the deletion.
