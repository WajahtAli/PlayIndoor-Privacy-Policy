Play Indoor ("Company", "We", "Us", or "Our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application **Play Indoor** (the "Application" or "Service"), available on the Google Play Store (Package Name: `app.play.indoor`).

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, accessing, or using Play Indoor, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not access or use the Application.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information collected automatically during app usage, and information gathered via device permissions.

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information:** When you register, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and optional profile picture.
* **Authentication Data:** Credentials used to sign up or sign in (via email/password or third-party identity providers such as Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In).
* **Booking & Session Data:** Details of sports grounds, courts, facilities booked, booking dates/times, duration, and session preferences.
* **Community & Feed Content:** Content you voluntarily create and post, including venue reviews, ratings, stories, questions, and responses.
* **Customer Support:** Inquiries, feedback, and support communications sent to our help desk.

### B. Financial & Host Payout Information
* **Players / Customers:** When making a booking, payment transaction records (such as transaction IDs, timestamps, booking amounts, and payment status) are stored for order fulfillment and receipt generation. Sensitive payment card numbers are processed directly by certified payment gateways; **Play Indoor does not process or store complete credit/debit card numbers on its servers.**
* **Hosts / Venue Partners (Direct Earnings Payouts):** To transfer booking revenue directly to venue owners and hosts without third-party administration delays, we collect host payout details, including:
  * Bank Account Title / Beneficiary Name
  * Bank Name and Branch Code
  * Account Number or International Bank Account Number (IBAN)
  * Authorized Mobile Wallet / Digital Payment Identifiers (e.g., JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or regional equivalents)
  
  This information is used strictly to disburse booking earnings directly to the host's account and for compliance with applicable accounting and tax standards.

### C. Device Permissions and Automatically Collected Information
Play Indoor requests access to specific device features only when required for core application functionality:

| Feature / Permission | Purpose & Usage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Approximate & Precise Location** (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) | Used while the app is actively in use to discover nearby sports grounds, calculate real-time distance to courts, and display interactive maps. **We do not track your location in the background when the app is closed.** |
| **Camera** (`CAMERA`) | Used with your explicit permission to capture profile pictures, take photos/videos of sports venues (for hosts), attach photos to stories/posts, and scan booking-related codes. |
| **Photos & Media Library** (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) | Used solely to allow you to select and upload images (profile pictures, venue listings, story updates) and save digital booking receipts to your device. We do not access photos or videos outside those you specifically choose. |
| **Biometric Authentication** (`USE_BIOMETRIC`) | Used optionally for secure, fast biometric login (Fingerprint / Face ID). **Biometric data is processed entirely on your local device hardware by the operating system.** We never receive, process, or store biometric samples on our servers. |
| **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | Used to send transactional booking confirmations, slot reminders, venue status updates, and direct host payout notifications. You can disable notifications at any time in device settings. |
| **Diagnostic & Device Data** | Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and crash reports collected automatically for system diagnostics and fraud prevention. |

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:
1. **Service Delivery:** Facilitating sports venue discovery, booking reservations, schedule management, and ground listings.
2. **Direct Host Payouts:** Calculating and transferring net booking revenues directly into the host's registered bank or payment account.
3. **Transaction Receipts & Verification:** Generating digital booking receipts and verification tokens for entry at indoor sports venues.
4. **Communications:** Delivering booking confirmations, schedule changes, customer support responses, and security notifications via push notifications, email, or SMS.
5. **Platform Safety & Anti-Fraud:** Verifying authentic users, preventing double-bookings, preventing payment fraud, and securing platform integrity.
6. **Performance & Analytics:** Monitoring crash rates, app stability, and improving user interface workflows.

## 4. How Information is Shared

We share your information strictly in the following scenarios:
* **Between Players and Venue Hosts:** When a booking is finalized, relevant reservation details (player name, contact phone number, and booked time slot) are shared with the venue host to validate entry.
* **Between Hosts and Customers:** Public venue information (ground name, address, pricing, available facilities, and operating hours) is visible to all registered users.
* **With Trusted Service Providers:** Third-party cloud infrastructure and SMS/email delivery providers who process data strictly under our contractual instructions and confidentiality agreements.
* **Legal & Regulatory Obligations:** If required by law, court order, or governmental authorities to protect rights, property, or safety.
* **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, customer and host records may be transferred with prior notice.

## 5. Data Retention Policy

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and satisfy legal, accounting, and reporting obligations:
* **Active User & Host Accounts:** Retained for the lifetime of your account.
* **Booking & Financial Records:** Retained for up to 36 months following transaction completion to handle customer disputes, audit trails, and financial compliance.
* **Chat, Support & Feed Content:** Retained while the associated account remains active or until manually deleted by the user.
* **Technical Logs & Diagnostic Data:** Automatically deleted or aggregated/anonymized within 12 months.

## 7. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures, including:
* End-to-end TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the mobile app and cloud servers.
* Role-based access control and strict Firebase Security Rules ensuring users can only read and write authorized records.
* Tokenized payment processing so sensitive card numbers never touch our servers.

While we take every reasonable measure to protect your data, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.
