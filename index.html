Privacy Policy for Play Indoor

Application: Play Indoor (app.play.indoor) Effective Date: September 23, 2026 Last Updated: September 23, 2026

Play Indoor ("Company", "We", "Us", or "Our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal data when you use the Play Indoor mobile application (the "Application" or "Service"), available on Google Play (Package Name: app.play.indoor).

By accessing or using Play Indoor, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, data generated automatically, and information obtained via device permissions necessary for core sports venue booking and management features.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Profile: Full name, email address, phone number, and optional profile picture.
  • Authentication Credentials: Secure authentication tokens and credentials when signing in via email/password, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In.
  • Bookings & Schedules: Sports ground details, court selections, reservation dates, start/end times, and playing preferences.
  • Community & Feed Posts: Reviews, ratings, community feed posts, venue stories, and user responses shared voluntarily.
  • Support Inquiries: In-app customer support requests, error reports, and communication records.

B. Financial Data & Host Direct Payout Information

  • Players / Customers: We record transaction confirmations, digital booking receipt IDs, timestamps, and order statuses. Sensitive payment card numbers are processed directly by certified third-party payment gateways; Play Indoor does not process, retain, or store full credit or debit card numbers on its servers.
  • Hosts / Venue Partners (Direct Earnings Payouts): To transfer booking revenues directly to venue owners without administrative delays, we collect host payout details, including:
    • Account Title / Beneficiary Name
    • Bank Name and Branch Code
    • Account Number / International Bank Account Number (IBAN)
    • Designated Mobile Wallet / Digital Payment Identifiers (e.g., JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or regional equivalents)
    This financial information is used strictly to disburse booking earnings directly into the host's account and to fulfill accounting and tax compliance requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information strictly for legitimate app functionality and legal obligations:

  • Fulfill Reservations: Processing court reservations, slot scheduling, and generating entrance passes.
  • Direct Host Payouts: Transferring earned booking revenue directly to venue host accounts.
  • Customer Communication: Sending transactional alerts, booking receipts, and policy notices.
  • Security & Anti-Fraud: Verifying genuine accounts, preventing double-bookings, and preventing unauthorized payment attempts.
  • Performance Optimization: Monitoring stability, diagnostic logs, and resolving technical crashes.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties or marketing brokers. Data is shared strictly as follows:

  • Between Players and Hosts: When a reservation is placed, the player's name and contact number are shared with the host to validate entrance and manage field access.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting, messaging, and payout infrastructure partners bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Legal Mandates: Where required by valid law, subpoena, or competent regulatory authority.

5. Data Retention Policy

We retain personal information only for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account Information: Maintained for the lifetime of your active account.
  • Booking and Financial Records: Maintained for up to 36 months to resolve billing inquiries, reconcile direct payouts, and satisfy statutory tax/audit obligations.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Kept for a maximum of 12 months before automatic deletion or aggregation.
